## 4. Engineering & Material Specifications

### Structural Framework
- **Earthquake Resilience:** Monolithic reinforced concrete frame engineered in strict compliance with the latest BNBC (Bangladesh National Building Code) seismic and wind-load criteria.
- **Structural Steel:** 60-grade, 500 WOTMT high-strength deformed rebar from top-rated domestic steel makers: **BSRM**, **AKS**, or **KSRM**.
- **Cement:** High-grade Portland cement from **Crown**, **Bashundhara**, **Shah Cement**, or **Holcim**.
- **Foundations:** Deep pile foundation engineered to soil test survey reports with high-tolerance concrete mixes.

### Floor & Stair Finishes
- **Living, Dining, Bedrooms & Balconies:** Premium local mirror-polished homogeneous tiles (**24" × 24"**).
- **Lobby & Stairs:** Mirror-polished tiles across main entrance lobby, intermediate landings, and risers with protected anti-slip grooved nosing.

### Bathroom Appointments
- **Sanitary Ware:** Top-tier porcelain commodes, counter-top basins, soap cases, towel rails, and paper holders (**RAK-Karla** or approved equivalent).
- **Wall & Floor Tiles:** Full-height glazed tiles up to 7.0 feet with designer decorative borders (16" × 16" floor tiles; 24" × 12" wall tiles by **RAK** / **Sheltech**).
- **CP Fittings:** Premium chrome-plated brass fittings and bib cocks (**Sattar** / **Haibali** / equivalent).
- **Mirrors & Lighting:** Large beveled mirrors with dedicated overhead illuminated fixtures.

### Kitchen Features
- **Countertop:** In-situ concrete platform at 2.5 ft elevation topped with selected **Green Marble** slab.
- **Wall Glazing:** Continuous glazed tiles with border up to 7.0 ft height above working counter (**Sheltech** / **RAK**).
- **Floor Tiles:** Homogeneous tiles (16" × 16") in kitchen floor and verandah; wall tiles 24" × 12".
- **Sink & Fixtures:** Single-bowl stainless steel countertop sink with gooseneck sink cock (**Sweet Home - Thailand**).
- **Ventilation:** Provision for heavy-duty exhaust fan and double-burner gas connection.

### Mechanical & Electrical Systems
- **Elevators:** **02 (Two) high-speed China-standard passenger lifts** (8 passengers capacity each):
  - Advanced AC-VVF drive system ensuring smooth acceleration and braking
  - Full-height infrared safety photocell sensors
  - Emergency alarm and automatic rescue device (ARD)
  - Well-furnished decorative stainless steel cabins
- **Emergency Standby Generators:**
  - One heavy-duty emergency generator providing backup for:
    - Both passenger lifts
    - Water pumps
    - Common corridor, staircase, and driveway lighting
    - Inside each apartment: 1 light + 1 fan in 3 beds, living, and dining; 1 light in kitchen and 3 bathrooms; calling bell and intercom.
  - Secondary substitute generator for uninterrupted essential utility backup.
- **Water Supply:** Two best-quality Italian water pumps (**Pedrollo / Saer - Italy**; one operational + one standby).
- **Intercom:** Elaborate PABX intercom network connecting reception, guard post, and all apartments.
- **Fire Safety:** Engineered firefighting system compliant with international safety law, equipped with fire hydrants, hose reels, and chemical extinguishers on every floor.
- **Management & Security:** Dedicated on-site management office, security guard quarters with kitchen, supervisor room, driver sitting lounge, and dedicated toilets.

## 5. Commercial Terms & Payment Schedule

1. **Booking & Allotment:**
   - Formal application on prescribed company form accompanied by minimum **30% booking money** of total apartment value.
   - Non-accepted applications receive 100% booking refund within 1 (one) month.
2. **Payments:**
   - All installments, car park allocations, and fees payable via demand draft, pay order, or crossed check favoring **Avenue Constructions Limited**.
3. **Late Payments:**
   - Delay in scheduled installment payment incurs a statutory late fee of 20% per 60 days.
   - Delays beyond 60 days entitle the developer to cancel allotment; refunded after resale to a new buyer less 10% deduction of total flat value.
4. **Deed of Agreement:**
   - Bilateral formal deed of agreement executed immediately after down payment.
5. **Title & Land Share Registration:**
   - Undivided, proportionate share of land registered in buyer's name at buyer's cost upon receipt of 100% payment.
6. **Allotment Transfer Fee:**
   - Post-handover transfer processing fee to third parties: **Tk. 2,00,000/-** plus all government tax/registration costs.
7. **Utility Service Facilities Cost:**
   - Fixed allocation for gas, water, sewerage, and electricity sub-station connection: **Tk. 4,00,000/-**.
8. **Apartment Owner's Association:**
   - Formation of cooperative society upon project delivery. Mandatory one-time membership fund: **Tk. 50,000/-**.
9. **Project Timeline:**
   - Construction start: October, 2026. Handover: October, 2029 (36 Months).
