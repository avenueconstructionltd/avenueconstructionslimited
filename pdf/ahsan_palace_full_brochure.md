## 4. Engineering & Material Specifications

### Structural Framework
- **Seismic Resilience:** Earthquake-resistant reinforced cement concrete (RCC) framed structure designed according to BNBC (Bangladesh National Building Code) standards.
- **Structural Steel:** 60-grade, 500 WOTMT high-yield de-formed steel reinforcement from premier mills: **BSRM**, **AKS**, or **KSRM**.
- **Cement:** Top-tier Portland Composite Cement from **Crown**, **Bashundhara**, **Shah Cement**, or **Holcim**.
- **Aggregates & Sand:** First-class picked jhama brick aggregates/crushed stone aggregate and coarse Sylhet sand (FM 2.5) for high-strength casting.

### Floor & Lobby Finishes
- **Apartment Floors & Verandahs:** Local mirror-polished homogeneous tiles (**24" x 24"**).
- **Staircases & Landing:** Mirror-polished floor tiles in building lobby, stair landings, and risers with protected grooved non-slip nosing.

### Bathroom Appointments
- **Sanitary Ware:** Premier local porcelain fixtures including commode, wash basin, soap case, towel rail, and paper holder (**RAK-Karla** or approved equivalent).
- **Wall & Floor Tiles:** Glazed ceramic wall tiles with border and decorative inserts up to full ceiling height (16" x 16" floor tiles; 24" x 12" wall tiles by **RAK** / **Sheltech**).
- **Fittings & Accessories:** Premium chrome-plated (C.P.) fittings by **Sattar** / **Haibali** / equivalent.
- **Lighting & Mirrors:** Large clear beveled-edge mirrors with overhead dedicated warm vanity lamps.

### Kitchen Execution
- **Countertop:** In-situ concrete platform at 2.5 ft elevation topped with selected **Green Marble** worktop.
- **Wall Glazing:** Continuous glazed tiles with decorative border extending up to 7.0 ft height above counter.
- **Flooring:** Non-slip homogeneous tiles (16" x 16" by RAK/Sheltech); Wall tiles 24" x 12".
- **Sink & Fittings:** Single bowl stainless steel countertop sink with high-neck sink cock (**Sweet Home - Thailand**).
- **Ventilation & Gas:** Suitably placed heavy-duty exhaust fan; provisions for double-burner gas connection.

### Electrical & Mechanical Systems
- **Passenger Elevator:** 1 (One) European standard high-speed 8-passenger elevator equipped with:
  - Microprocessor-based AC-VVF drive system for whisper-quiet travel
  - Full-height safety photocell beam
  - Emergency alarm and automatic rescue device (ARD)
  - Luxuriously appointed stainless steel cabin interior
- **Emergency Standby Generator:** Heavy-duty acoustic generator supplying emergency power to:
  - Passenger lift and dual water pumps
  - Lighting across all common corridors, lobbies, and staircases
  - Backup points inside every apartment: 1 light + 1 fan in 3 bedrooms, living, and dining; 1 light each in kitchen and 3 bathrooms; calling bell and intercom.
  - Substitute secondary generator support.
- **Water Distribution:** Dual best-quality Italian water pumps (**Pedrollo / Saer - Italy**), with one pump on active duty and one on standby.
- **Communications:** Central PABX intercom network connecting the reception desk, main entrance guard post, and each individual residence.
- **Fire Safety:** Engineered firefighting system compliant with international safety fire code, incorporating dry chemical fire extinguishers and dedicated fire hydrants.

## 5. Commercial Terms & Allotment Policy

1. **Booking & Allotment:**
   - Formal booking on prescribed company application form with minimum **30% booking money** of total apartment value.
   - Non-accepted applications receive 100% booking refund within 7 (seven) days.
2. **Payment Mode:**
   - Demand draft, pay order, or crossed account payee check in favor of **Avenue Constructions Limited**. Foreign remittances processed at official Bangladesh Bank conversion rates.
3. **Delay in Payments:**
   - Payment delayed beyond scheduled due dates incurs a late fee of 20% per 60 days.
   - Delays exceeding 60 days entitle the developer to cancel allotment; refunded after re-sale less 10% deduction of total flat value.
4. **Deed of Agreement:**
   - Formal bilateral deed executed immediately upon receipt of down payment to safeguard mutual rights.
5. **Possession & Proportionate Land Share:**
   - Undivided, demarcated proportionate share of land registered in buyer's name at buyer's cost upon full payment.
6. **Transfer to Third Party:**
   - No pre-handover transfers without company consent. Post-handover transfer processing fee is **Tk. 2,00,000/-** plus all statutory government documentation fees.
7. **Apartment Owner's Association:**
   - Mandatory cooperative society formed by all owners upon handover. One-time association reserve fee: **Tk. 50,000/-**.
8. **Utility Connection & Service Charges:**
   - Fixed allocation for gas, water, sewerage, and electricity sub-station connections: **Tk. 4,00,000/-**.
9. **Timeline:**
   - Commencement: May, 2026. Target handover: May, 2028 (24 months duration).
